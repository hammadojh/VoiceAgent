The model

Meet Miso One

The most emotive voice model in the world — an 8-billion-parameter text-to-speech system for highly expressive, conversational speech.

A text-to-dialogue RVQ Transformer

Inspired by the Sesame CSM architecture, Miso One pairs a Llama-style backbone with an autoregressive audio decoder to generate rich conversational speech from text and optional audio context.

Voice conditioning, conversation-history awareness, and built-in audio watermarking — engineered for responsible, production-grade voice.
